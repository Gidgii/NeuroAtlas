Educational use & disclaimer

Last reviewed: 21 August 2026

Educational resource

Neuro Atlas provides educational information about neuroscience, neuropsychology, EEG/qEEG, neurofeedback, trauma and related clinical topics. It is not medical, psychological, diagnostic or treatment advice.

The Atlas is not a substitute for individual assessment, professional judgement, formal training, supervision, current clinical guidelines or consultation with an appropriately qualified health professional.

Clinical use

Users remain responsible for independently verifying information before applying it in assessment, diagnosis, treatment, teaching, supervision or other professional activity. Neuro Atlas should not be used as the sole basis for a clinical decision.

Accuracy and changing evidence

Neuro Atlas is reviewed and evidence-linked, but neuroscience and clinical evidence continue to develop. Content may contain errors, omissions, simplifications or information that becomes outdated.

If you identify a possible error, outdated statement or technical problem, feedback is welcomed so the material can be reviewed and corrected where appropriate.

Patient and client information

Do not enter patient/client names, dates of birth, case notes, health records or other identifying or sensitive information into Neuro Atlas. The current release does not require patient information.

Acknowledgement record

The current release stores acknowledgement of this notice locally on the user's device, including the notice version and acceptance time. It is not transmitted to a Neuro Atlas account or server in this release and does not independently verify the identity of the person using the device.
