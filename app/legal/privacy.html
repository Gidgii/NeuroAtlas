Release information

Privacy

Last reviewed: 11 August 2026

What the Atlas stores

The application stores learning state such as progress, bookmarks, review scheduling, mastery and confidence-calibration data in browser storage on the device being used. The shipped application does not require an account and does not send this learning state to an Atlas application server.

What the Atlas does not need

The Atlas is an educational resource. Do not enter names, dates of birth, case notes, health records or other identifying patient/client information into the application. No feature requires patient information.

Hosting and external links

The web host may receive ordinary request information needed to serve the application, such as network address, browser information and requested files, according to the host's own practices. Evidence Library links open third-party websites, which apply their own privacy policies.

Analytics

The P5 release exposes local browser events that can support future analytics integration, but it does not itself transmit those events to an analytics provider. Any future telemetry implementation must update this notice before collection is enabled.

Australian privacy governance

If the Atlas later becomes an APP entity or begins collecting personal information, its public privacy policy and operational practices should be reviewed against the Australian Privacy Principles and other applicable law. This page describes the software behaviour of this release; it is not legal advice or a representation that every deployment is subject to the same obligations.
