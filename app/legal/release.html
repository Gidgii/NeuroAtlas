Release governance

This release packages the Atlas as an evidence-governed educational product rather than treating a passing build as proof of scientific certainty.

Evidence standard

Factual production concepts must remain linked to evidence metadata. Clinical recommendations, diagnostic inference, biomarkers, treatment effects and contested mechanisms require stronger contemporary sourcing and explicit boundaries. Source linkage means relevance to a concept; it does not imply that one source independently proves every sentence.

Review status

The evidence process is AI-assisted and includes automated integrity gates. It is not independent human academic peer review. Content that will be marketed for clinical education should retain a pathway for qualified external subject-matter review.

Educational scope

Neuro Atlas supports learning and clinical reasoning. It is not a diagnostic device, a treatment protocol, or a substitute for professional judgement, supervision, formal training, local policy or current clinical guidelines.

P5 product safeguards

  • Search scoping across major Atlas domains.
  • Privacy and accessibility disclosures available from the application.
  • Installability metadata and offline-cache coverage.
  • Local-only analytics hooks; no telemetry vendor enabled by default.
  • Automated release-readiness audit and regression tests.

P6–P7 learning safeguards

  • Advanced assessment rationales resolve to Evidence Library source IDs and remediation concepts.
  • Confidence is recorded before feedback so calibration errors can be distinguished from knowledge gaps.
  • Educator pathways sequence existing Atlas content rather than creating new diagnostic or treatment claims.
  • Learner reports remain on-device unless the user deliberately exports JSON, CSV or print output.
  • Assessment is educational and is not a credentialing examination or clinical decision-support system.
