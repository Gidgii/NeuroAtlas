# P1.5 — Healthy vs pathology clinical comparison

P1.5 adds a reusable side-by-side comparison lab to the system explorer. It is designed to make the atlas teach mechanism rather than merely name structures.

## Interaction pattern
- select a clinically relevant comparison;
- view **Healthy / expected** beside **Pathology / disrupted**;
- compare mechanism and observable presentation;
- deliberately reveal the deeper clinical interpretation;
- connect the comparison to assessment reasoning;
- retain an explicit caveat so the atlas does not imply one-to-one biological diagnosis.

## Initial coverage
The comparison lab is configured for:
- Healthy Versus Pathology Comparison;
- Lesion and Symptom Mapping;
- Brainstem Atlas;
- Basal Ganglia Circuit Explorer;
- Major White-Matter Pathways Atlas.

The main view stays brief. The deeper interpretation is hidden until requested, preserving the atlas' concise-first design.
