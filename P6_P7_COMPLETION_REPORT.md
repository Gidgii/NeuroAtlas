# Neuro Atlas P6–P7 — Assessment & Productisation

P6–P7 adds an evidence-linked assessment and educator layer without changing the locked entrance or rewriting production concept science.

## P6 — advanced assessment

- 18 cumulative clinical-neuroscience reasoning cases across seven domains.
- Localisation, neuropsychological interpretation, EEG/qEEG, neurofeedback, trauma/EMDR, neurodevelopment and integrated reasoning.
- Adaptive case selection prioritises unseen and weaker cases.
- Confidence is recorded before feedback so high-confidence errors can be surfaced.
- Every rationale links back to Atlas concepts for remediation and to Evidence Library source IDs.
- Diagnostic, biomarker and treatment questions explicitly teach scope limits rather than reverse inference.

## P7 — educator/product tooling

- Seven structured learning pathways.
- Local pathway assignment on the learner device.
- Portable JSON and CSV learner-report exports plus print support.
- No automatic learner-data transmission and no network analytics provider.
- Existing local progress and advanced-assessment results are summarised without introducing accounts or patient data.

## Evidence governance

P6–P7 uses the existing Evidence Library as the source of truth. Assessment cases fail automated audit if an evidence ID or remediation concept does not resolve. Current high-risk examples are anchored to the existing NICE PTSD guideline record, AAN qEEG/ADHD advisory, 2025 ADHD neurofeedback meta-analysis, ASD neurofeedback review and the Atlas's balanced Polyvagal/intergenerational-trauma records.

This remains an educational product, not a diagnostic device, clinical decision-support system, credentialing examination or substitute for supervision.
