# P1.6 — Assessment-linked clinical reasoning

## Purpose
Move the Atlas from localisation and pathology comparison into explicit hypothesis testing: **what should I assess first, what would support the hypothesis, and what would make me reconsider it?**

## Added
- Interactive assessment-priority choices with immediate feedback.
- Structured observation targets and task types.
- Findings that strengthen versus challenge a localisation/network hypothesis.
- Next-step guidance and explicit caveats.
- Links each case back to the relevant anatomical component when a spatial/explorer part is available.

## Initial coverage
- Brainstem
- Basal ganglia
- Major white-matter pathways
- Lesion and symptom mapping
- Healthy versus pathology comparison

## Guardrails
The lab deliberately avoids reverse-inference from test score to lesion or diagnosis. Assessment findings are framed as converging or disconfirming evidence within a broader neurological, developmental and functional context.
