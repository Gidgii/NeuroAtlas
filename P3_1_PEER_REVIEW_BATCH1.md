## Findings

### `polyvagal-theory` — SUBSTANTIVE
- Finding: Existing caution was directionally sound, but generic PTSD guidelines did not support theory-specific neuroanatomical/evolutionary claims.
- Action: Reframed as active scientific dispute and added both critical and proponent sources.

### `current-evidence-emdr` — MINOR
- Finding: Treatment-efficacy framing was broadly accurate but references did not capture newer comparative evidence or mechanism boundary.
- Action: Added 2024 IPD meta-analysis and explicit treatment-versus-mechanism distinction.

### `adhd-neurofeedback` — NO ISSUE
- Finding: Current wording matches the 2025 high-quality meta-analysis.
- Action: Verified and retained; normalised source metadata.

### `autism-neurofeedback` — SUBSTANTIVE
- Finding: An ADHD neurofeedback meta-analysis was being used as disorder-specific evidence for autism.
- Action: Replaced with an ASD-specific 2025 systematic review and retained cautious neurodiversity-safe framing.

### `qeeg-adhd-evidence` — SUBSTANTIVE
- Finding: Generic EEG/QEEG sources were insufficient for the ADHD diagnostic claim.
- Action: Added the AAN theta/beta-ratio practice advisory, reaffirmed in 2025.

### `qeeg-tbi-evidence` — SUBSTANTIVE
- Finding: Generic sources were insufficient for mTBI diagnostic limitations.
- Action: Added the ACNS qEEG mTBI practice guideline directly.

### `memory-reconsolidation-trauma` — SUBSTANTIVE
- Finding: The text was cautious but references were generic PTSD guidelines rather than reconsolidation evidence.
- Action: Added reconsolidation-specific systematic review and strengthened protocol/risk-of-bias qualification.

### `working-memory-theory-emdr` — SUBSTANTIVE
- Finding: Mechanism claims were plausible but supported only by generic PTSD guidelines.
- Action: Added a working-memory/EMDR systematic review and clarified that it is a component mechanism, not a complete account.

### `intergenerational-trauma` — SUBSTANTIVE
- Finding: Wording was already cautious, but evidence trail did not distinguish observed intergenerational associations from specifically epigenetic transmission.
- Action: Added current systematic/scoping reviews and strengthened confounding/causal limits.

## Evidence Library

`app/data/evidence-library.json` is the structured source register for the future in-app Evidence Library. It stores source type, DOI/PMID where available, evidence strength, status, limitations and concept links.

## Editorial rule

Treatment efficacy, mechanism plausibility, diagnostic utility and anatomical fact are reviewed as different claim types. A source that supports one is not automatically accepted as evidence for another.
